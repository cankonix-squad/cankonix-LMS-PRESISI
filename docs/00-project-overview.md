# Project Overview

## Goal
Enterprise LMS for Lemdiklat Polri, usable by many educational institutions. Participants are Polri members. Educators may work across institutions/programs. Supports face-to-face, online, blended, synchronous and asynchronous learning, broad assessment/exam capabilities, and national executive drill-down.

## Portals
- Admin — organization, users, authorization, academic configuration.
- Educator — classes, learning content, attendance, assignments, question bank, exams, grading.
- Student — learning, assignment, attendance, exams, grades, certificates; mobile-first.
- Executive — read-heavy KPI/reporting and national drill-down.

## Delivery phases
0 Repository Foundation; 1 Identity/Organization/RBAC; 2 Academic Core; 3 Learning; 4 Attendance; 5 Assessment/Exam; 6 Grading/Graduation/Certificate; 7 Executive Reporting.
